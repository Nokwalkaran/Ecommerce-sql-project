# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behaviour Analysis Using SQL

Synthetic portfolio dataset for SQL business analysis.

## Dataset
- 500 customers
- 3,000 orders
- 98 products
- 227 return records
- Orders cover January–December 2025

## Analysis areas
Revenue, AOV, category/product performance, customer segmentation, repeat customers,
MoM growth, rankings, profitability, inactive customers, return rates, Pareto analysis and cohort analysis.

## SQL skills
JOINs, CTEs, CASE WHEN, subqueries, aggregations, date functions and window functions
including RANK, ROW_NUMBER and LAG.

## Management Insights 
-HP Pavilion 14 generated the highest revenue among the products analyzed, 
followed by Wakefit Mattress among the leading products in their respective categories.

–Punjab is the highest-revenue-generating state with ₹43.62 lakh in revenue, followed by Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat. 
 This indicates that these states are key contributors to overall sales.

-Meera Gill is the highest-value customer with ₹711,672 in revenue, followed by Kavya Verma and Manav Joshi.
 These customers represent important high-value segments for retention and loyalty strategies. 
 
-81.59% of customers are repeat customers, 
 indicating a strong level of customer retention and repeat-purchase behaviour. 
 
-23 of 98 products generate approximately 80.18% of total revenue,
 highlighting a concentrated revenue contribution from a relatively small group of products. 

-Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the year, with the highest revenue recorded in August at ₹25.66 lakh. The largest month-over-month increase occurred in June (+83.49%), 
 while May recorded the lowest monthly revenue at ₹11.78 lakh. 
 
-8.25% of orders were returned,indicating that a measurable portion of completed orders resulted in returns. 
The company should investigate return reasons and identify products or categories with higher return rates to reduce future returns. 

- Customers who have not purchased in the last 90 days but have previously generated significant revenue should be prioritized for retention campaigns. Targeting these inactive high-value customers with personalized offers, loyalty incentives,
 or product recommendations could help encourage repeat purchases.
